You deserve support from a real person. In India, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 or 1-800-891-4416 (free, 24×7). In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, see findahelpline.com. In the app, the same numbers are under Settings › If you're going through something.

Is anyone in the app real?

No. Every account, reply, photo and voice note is simulated or AI-generated on your iPhone, apart from @premponguru, the account of the person who made the app. Those photos are real, and that account's messages were written in advance. You can't message that account, and nobody sees what you write. If you ask anyone in the app whether they're real, they will tell you they aren't.

Report, block and mute

Long-press or open the menu on any post, comment, message or profile and choose Report. The item is hidden on your phone straight away. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose Email us. You can also Block or Mute any account.

Spicy chats

This setting is off by default and only appears for adults. Chats stay non-explicit either way. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Starting over

Settings › Erase everything removes your posts, chats and followers from this iPhone for good. Deleting the app does the same.
