Famous-ish

A fame simulator for iPhone · Adults only

Famous-ish is an offline, single-player game about being internet famous. You post, and a simulated crowd reacts: likes, comments, fan wars, polls and DMs.

Nobody in it is real. Every account, reply, photo and voice note is simulated or AI-generated on your phone, apart from @premponguru, the account of the person who made the app. Those photos are real, and that account's messages were written in advance. Nothing you write reaches anyone.
