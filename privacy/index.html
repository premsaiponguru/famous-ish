Privacy policy

Famous-ish · 18+ · Effective date: 6 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect no data. Nothing you write or do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Everything stays on your iPhone, in the app's own protected storage. Settings › Erase everything removes it for good.
  • No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Who we are

Famous-ish is an app made by Prem Sai Ponguru, an independent developer. In this policy "we" means Prem Sai Ponguru. You can reach us at premsai.ponguru@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

Your profile, your posts, the photos you choose to add, your chats and the simulated reactions are saved only on your device. The app has no server to send them to, so we cannot see them, and neither can anyone else.

Deleting the app, or using Settings › Erase everything, removes all of it for good.

How replies and photos are handled

Replies are chosen from lines written in advance. On iPhones that support it, some everyday replies are written by Apple's on-device language model, on your iPhone. Flirting never goes to the model. When you add a photo, Apple's on-device image tools read it on your iPhone so the crowd can react to it. Neither of these sends your words or photos to us.

Photos are added through Apple's photo picker, so the app only receives the ones you choose. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Your age

Famous-ish is for adults. The app asks you to confirm you are 18 or over and may use Apple's Declared Age Range feature, where available and if you agree to share it. The answer is used on your iPhone to decide which mode the app runs in. It is not sent to us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing is pushed from a server.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites. Apple gives every developer some general statistics, such as download counts, and crash reports from people who chose to share them in their iPhone settings. These contain nothing that identifies you to us.

Links that leave the app

Only a link you tap goes online, such as Contact, Support, this page or Instagram. It opens outside the app, and that site's or app's own policy applies there. A helpline number you tap opens your phone app.

Children

Famous-ish is not for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you tell the app you are under 18, it stops all romance and switches to a friendly mode. If you believe a child has e-mailed us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

If you e-mail us

If you write to us, including through Report › Email us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you include. We use them only to reply to you. We do not share them or add you to any mailing list, and we delete them when the conversation is over.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because the app sends us nothing, the only thing we could hold is an e-mail you sent us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If the app ever started to collect any data, we would update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
